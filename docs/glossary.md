# Glossary

Key terms used across this research. Updated as new concepts are encountered.

---

## Quantum Biology

**Quantum coherence** — A quantum mechanical property where a system exists in a superposition of states simultaneously. In photosynthesis, this may allow excitation energy to explore multiple transfer pathways at once, improving efficiency.

**Decoherence** — The loss of quantum properties due to interaction with the environment. The central puzzle of quantum biology is how biological systems maintain coherence long enough to be functional at warm, wet, noisy temperatures.

**FMO complex (Fenna-Matthews-Olson)** — A pigment-protein complex found in green sulfur bacteria. The most studied quantum photosynthetic structure; acts as a quantum wire transferring excitation energy from the chlorosome antenna to the reaction centre. PDB ID: `3ENI`.

**Exciton** — A bound state of an electron and an electron hole, or more loosely, a mobile unit of electronic excitation energy. In photosynthesis, excitons carry absorbed solar energy through the light-harvesting complex.

**Light-harvesting complex (LHC)** — Protein structures in photosynthetic organisms that absorb photons and funnel excitation energy toward reaction centres.

**Reaction centre** — The part of the photosynthetic system where absorbed light energy is converted into chemical energy (charge separation).

**Quantum yield** — The ratio of useful output to energy input. Natural photosynthetic energy transfer achieves near-unity quantum yield — almost every absorbed photon results in a productive energy transfer event.

## Quantum Computing

**Qubit** — The basic unit of quantum information. Unlike classical bits (0 or 1), qubits can exist in superpositions of both.

**Quantum superposition** — A qubit (or quantum system) existing in multiple states simultaneously until measured.

**Quantum entanglement** — A correlation between qubits where the state of one is instantaneously related to another, regardless of distance.

**VQE (Variational Quantum Eigensolver)** — A hybrid quantum-classical algorithm for finding the ground state energy of a molecule. One of the most promising near-term quantum chemistry algorithms.

**Haken-Ströbl model** — A simplified model of quantum energy transfer in photosynthetic systems. Treats the environment as white noise (Markovian). Used in the Guimarães 2020 IBM Q simulation.

**Lindblad master equation** — The standard equation governing open quantum systems (quantum systems interacting with an environment). Used to model photosynthetic energy transfer including decoherence.

**T2 coherence time** — The timescale over which a qubit maintains quantum coherence. Current IBM Q systems: ~100 microseconds. FMO biological coherence: ~100–500 femtoseconds.

## Artificial Photosynthesis / Chemistry

**CO₂ reduction reaction (CO₂RR)** — The electrochemical or photochemical reduction of CO₂ into useful molecules: CO, methane (CH₄), methanol, ethylene, formic acid, jet fuel.

**Photocatalyst** — A substance that accelerates a chemical reaction when exposed to light. At the core of artificial photosynthesis systems.

**Electrolysis / Electrolyser** — A device that uses electrical energy to drive a non-spontaneous chemical reaction. Twelve's core technology is an electrolyser for CO₂ conversion.

**Nanomaterial biohybrid (NBH)** — A system combining nanomaterial photocatalysts or photosensitizers with biological enzymes or microorganisms to drive chemical reactions.

**Quantum efficiency** — In the context of artificial photosynthesis, the percentage of absorbed photons that result in a useful chemical reaction. Natural photosynthesis max: ~6%. Some artificial systems have exceeded 20%.
